Frequently Asked Questions

Clear answers regarding our engagement structures, confidentiality governance, and technical diagnostic processes.

How does STACKLY protect confidential corporate data during an assessment? expand_more
All proprietary data, financial statements, and technological topologies are isolated in dedicated zero-knowledge cryptographic customer enclaves. Our consultants operate under strict bilateral NDAs and zero-trust data access controls.
How long does a comprehensive enterprise risk audit take to complete? expand_more
A focused single-domain audit (such as a cyber attack surface or FX liquidity stress test) typically concludes within 3 to 4 weeks. Full enterprise ERM implementation across multiple global divisions spans 8 to 12 weeks.
Do you work with mid-market companies or exclusively Fortune 500 enterprises? expand_more
STACKLY structures advisory engagements for mid-market high-growth organizations (beginning around $50M in annual revenue) up to Fortune 100 conglomerates. We tailor the complexity of our quantitative models to match organizational operational scale.
Can STACKLY help our executive team prepare for upcoming board audit meetings? expand_more
Yes. We regularly produce board-ready slide decks, actuarial risk registers, and quantitative heat matrices that withstand tough audit committee interrogation.
How does STACKLY differ from the Big Four advisory firms? expand_more
Unlike traditional firms that deploy junior staff using generic compliance checklists, STACKLY pairs clients exclusively with seasoned former C-level risk commanders who build automated, continuous risk systems rather than static paper reports.
